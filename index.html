About

My academic interests include biophysics, quantum physics, and nuclear physics. I am especially drawn to interdisciplinary questions that bring together fundamental scientific principles, experimentation, and real-world applications.

My first research experience at the University of Miami helped me develop an appreciation for experimental design, data analysis, collaboration, and the persistence required to troubleshoot scientific problems. Outside the lab, I enjoy using communication and technology to create opportunities for students to share their ideas.

I hope to continue my studies in physics, contribute to meaningful research, and eventually pursue a career that combines scientific research with university teaching.

Research experience

My first research traineeship introduced me to the process of developing an experiment, working with data, and adapting when an approach did not work as expected.

01

University of Miami · Summer 2026

Investigating color preferences in Drosophila

As a summer research trainee, I contributed to a project investigating fruit flies' responses to different colors. My work included helping develop a 24-well experimental arena, assisting with the lighting and imaging setup, and working with image-analysis methods. The experience exposed me to experimental design, data collection, troubleshooting, and the importance of controlling variables in a research environment.

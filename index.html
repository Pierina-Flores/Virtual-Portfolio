Research Experience

University of Miami · Summer 2026

Drosophila Color Preference Research

During a summer research traineeship at the University of Miami, I contributed to a project investigating color preferences in Drosophila. The experience involved experimental design, equipment development, image analysis, and troubleshooting.

I worked with a 24-well arena setup, lighting configurations, camera systems, and MATLAB-based image analysis. I also helped address experimental challenges involving lighting consistency and environmental conditions.

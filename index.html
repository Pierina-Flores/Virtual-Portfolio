University of Miami Research Experience

Drosophila Color Preference Research

Participated in a summer research traineeship investigating color preferences in Drosophila. The project involved developing and refining experimental setups to study behavioral responses under controlled lighting conditions.

Contributed to the development of a 24-well experimental arena, worked with lighting and imaging configurations, and helped troubleshoot experimental variables that could influence observations. The experience introduced me to experimental design, image analysis, and the challenges of producing consistent research data.
